SOURCES & MODEL LIMITS

Mission dates and outcomes come from the catalog; specific route families use linked mission sources. All 3D coordinates are generated for explanation, with fixed Earth and Moon, enlarged bodies, compressed distances and simplified manoeuvres. No SPICE ephemerides, real velocities, landing coordinates, rover tracks or gravitational integration are used. This is a mission-stage animation, not an exact historical trajectory.

A NOTE ON THE RECORD

Exploration includes
the attempts.

This archive displays the 123 major mission and spacecraft records in NASA’s Moon Missions catalog, including failed attempts and separately listed rovers. These are records, not a count of unique launches. The catalog does not include every CubeSat, secondary payload, or lunar-related test flight.

Dates are in UTC, as recorded by the source. Arrival may mean a flyby, orbit insertion, landing, or impact. A successful impactor is a successful mission. Historic outcomes do not imply a spacecraft is still operating.

Catalog snapshot: 5 November 2026. Launch-date corrections are noted in the affected records. Independent educational project.
